# Customer Segmentation Analysis using K-Means Clustering
## Project Overview

This project applies unsupervised machine learning techniques to segment customers based on their purchasing behaviour, income levels, and engagement patterns. Using clustering methods, the analysis identifies distinct customer groups that exhibit different spending habits, channel preferences, and price sensitivity.

The goal of this project is to demonstrate how businesses can leverage customer segmentation to better understand their customer base and design targeted marketing strategies that improve engagement, retention, and revenue generation.

## Business Problem

Businesses often treat customers as a single homogeneous group, which can lead to ineffective marketing strategies and inefficient resource allocation. However, customers differ significantly in terms of purchasing behaviour, spending power, and engagement levels.

**This project aims to answer the following key questions:**

- Are there distinct customer segments within the customer base?

- How do these segments differ in terms of income, spending behaviour, and purchase frequency?

- Which segments contribute the most revenue to the business?

- How can businesses tailor marketing strategies for each segment?

## Five Distinct Customer Segments Identified: 

| Segment                  | Characteristics                                                               |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Premium Customers        | Highest income and order value, frequent purchasers, lowest price sensitivity |
| High Value Customers     | High purchasing power and strong engagement                                   |
| Young Online Shoppers    | Highly active online customers with moderate spending                         |
| Low Engagement Customers | Moderate income but low purchasing frequency                                  |
| Low Spending Customers   | Lowest order value and purchasing activity                                    |


## Key Insights

- Premium Customers generate the highest revenue despite being the smallest segment.

- High Value Customers represent a strong revenue-driving segment with significant purchasing power.

- Young Online Shoppers demonstrate strong digital engagement but moderate spending levels.

- Low Engagement and Low Spending Customers present opportunities for targeted promotions and reactivation strategies.

These insights highlight the importance of identifying high-value customers while developing tailored engagement strategies for lower-performing segments.
